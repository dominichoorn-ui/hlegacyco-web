Privacy Notice

HLegacy Co (Pty) Ltd · Registration no. 2024/129001/07 · Last updated 23 November 2026

We handle personal information carefully, because discretion is the core of our work. This notice explains what we collect, why, who sees it and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

HLegacy Co (Pty) Ltd ("HLegacy Co", "we") is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website and our services. Our Information Officer is the Managing Director, Dominic Hoorn. Contact: dominic@hlegacyco.com.

2. What we collect

  • When you contact us or complete the consultation form: your name, phone number (optional), the service you are interested in, and what you choose to tell us about your situation, concerns and questions.
  • When you engage us: the information needed to deliver the agreed service, set out in your written service agreement. This may include information about other people or companies relevant to your matter.
  • Website use: we do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Our web host and font provider may log technical data such as your IP address as part of normally serving the page.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a proposal.
  • To deliver the services you engage us for, under a written agreement.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

We process information on the basis of your consent, to conclude or perform a contract with you, to comply with the law, or to pursue a legitimate interest that does not override your rights. Information about third parties in a matter is processed only for the lawful purpose agreed with the client and within the limits of the law.

4. Who sees it

Your information is seen only by the people working on your matter. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us operate, such as email hosting and the service that forwards website form submissions to our inbox, under confidentiality;
  • where the law requires it, or with your consent.

Some of these service providers store data outside South Africa. Where that happens, we rely on providers bound by data-protection laws or agreements that give protection comparable to POPIA (section 72).

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 12 months unless you ask us to keep them.

6. How we protect it

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access limited to those who need it, secured accounts and confidential handling of case files. If we become aware of a security compromise affecting your information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or object to its processing, and you may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal limits. Email dominic@hlegacyco.com. We respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

8. Changes

We may update this notice. The date above shows the latest version.
