For bidders: what we see from the other side

Most bids aren’t lost on price.

They’re lost before anyone looks at the price. These five mistakes knock out capable companies every week, and every one of them is fixable before you submit.

Compliance documents missing or expiredTax status, CSD, B-BBEE and PSIRA certificates must be valid on the closing date.
SBD forms incomplete or unsignedBlank declarations, or a signatory without a resolution authorising them.
Compulsory briefing missedNo signature on the attendance register means no valid bid.
Pricing that breaks the rulesRates below the sectoral or bargaining-council minimum wage, or an altered schedule.
Functionality threshold not metA generic profile instead of evidence scored against each criterion.
