Security risk assessment

Know your real security risk

Security spend often grows after every incident, without anyone stepping back to ask what the real risks are, and what they could cost the business. A proper assessment looks at the site, the people, the procedures and the technology together, and tells you where the next loss is most likely to come from.

Book a scoping callAll security services

The concern

“We've had three incidents this year and our security spend keeps rising. Are we managing the right risks?”

What we assess

  • A structured risk assessment to international risk-management standards: threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood and business impact
  • The crime and threat baseline for your area, trended over several years
  • Physical survey: perimeter, lighting, sightlines, access points and environmental design
  • Procedures: incident response, key and access control, visitor and contractor management
  • Security systems: alarms, CCTV, access control and how well they are monitored and maintained
  • A prioritised risk register with owners, cost bands and timelines

Standard

The full assessment

  • A structured risk assessment to international risk-management standards: threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood and business impact
  • The crime and threat baseline for your area, trended over several years
  • Physical survey: perimeter, lighting, sightlines, access points and environmental design
  • Procedures: incident response, key and access control, visitor and contractor management

Premium

Everything in Standard, plus

  • Multi-site assessment: A portfolio view across your sites, with one consolidated board report
  • Board workshop: A facilitated session with your board or trustees on the findings and priorities
  • 90-day remediation review: A return visit to confirm what has been fixed and what still needs attention

How we work

Independent. Documented. Board-ready.

Step 1

Scoping call

A free, confidential call: your sites, your concerns and who decides. We confirm the scope, fee and timeline in writing.

Step 2

Written authorisation

Rules of engagement, and for any testing a signed letter of authorisation from everyone responsible for the spaces tested.

Step 3

Fieldwork

Desktop baseline, site visits, interviews, document and contract review, with every step recorded.

Step 4

Report and debrief

A board-ready report, a prioritised risk register and a remediation plan, presented to you in person, with a 30-day follow-up.

We act in good faith and hold every engagement to the Private Security Industry Regulation Act and the PSIRA Code of Conduct, the Protection of Personal Information Act, the Cybercrimes Act, RICA, the Labour Relations Act and the Trespass Act, so our findings and recommendations stand up to scrutiny. Scope is confirmed in a written service agreement.
