How we work

Independent. Documented. Board-ready.

Step 1

Scoping call

A free, confidential call: your sites, your concerns and who decides. We confirm the scope, fee and timeline in writing.

Step 2

Written authorisation

Rules of engagement, and for any testing a signed letter of authorisation from everyone responsible for the spaces tested.

Step 3

Fieldwork

Desktop baseline, site visits, interviews, document and contract review, with every step recorded.

Step 4

Report and debrief

A board-ready report, a prioritised risk register and a remediation plan, presented to you in person, with a 30-day follow-up.

We act in good faith and hold every engagement to the Private Security Industry Regulation Act and the PSIRA Code of Conduct, the Protection of Personal Information Act, the Cybercrimes Act, RICA, the Labour Relations Act and the Trespass Act, so our findings and recommendations stand up to scrutiny. Scope is confirmed in a written service agreement.

Security consultancy is provided by Dominic Hoorn, PSIRA-registered security service provider (Reg. 2976170). HLegacy Co sells no guarding, equipment or installations and takes no commission from providers.

Our limits

What we will not do.

Independence and discipline are what you are paying for. These lines hold on every engagement.

  • Sell you guarding, equipment or installations, or take commission from anyone we recommend
  • Run any test without written authorisation from everyone responsible for the space
  • Go beyond the authorised scope of any test, or intercept anyone's communications
  • Design staff monitoring that has no lawful basis or bypasses employee consent where it is required
  • Recommend measures that block emergency exits or compromise safety
  • Share your findings, site plans or vulnerabilities with anyone outside the engagement
