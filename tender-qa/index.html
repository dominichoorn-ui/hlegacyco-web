What the courts have made clear

Lessons from South African procurement judgments.

Our audits apply the standards that procurement judgments have set, so your bid is judged on its merit and not lost on a technicality.

A receipt is not a certificate

Proof that you applied for a renewal does not replace a valid certificate on the closing date. We treat it as a disqualification risk, not a minor gap.

Your price is final

Pricing errors cannot be corrected after submission, and a rate below the statutory minimum wage stays your risk long after the award. We check every line before you sign.

Ambiguity costs points

Evaluators score what is on the page. Every CV carries clear start and end dates, and every reference letter is on the client’s letterhead, with value, duration, scope and signature.

Every partner stands alone

In a joint venture or subcontracting bid, each participating entity must be compliant in its own right. We check every one of them, not only the lead bidder.

Your scorecard, fix list and guidance, in writing. You sign, finalise and submit, with every decision documented.

Tender quality assurance is a document-review service. Final signatures, statutory declarations and submission remain the responsibility of the bidder’s authorised signatory.

Our limits

What we will not do.

Your bid has to stand on its own merit. These lines protect it, and you.

  • Approach evaluators, committee members or procurement officials on your behalf
  • Act for more than one bidder on the same tender
  • Share your pricing, strategy or documents with anyone outside the engagement
  • Sign, certify or submit any document for you, or make statutory declarations on your behalf
  • Queue at government offices, attend briefings or deliver bids to tender boxes
  • Help a bid misrepresent capacity, experience or compliance
