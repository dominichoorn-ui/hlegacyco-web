Claims against us

Verify before you pay

A large claim arrives with a convincing story. Most claims are genuine. The ones that are not tend to share patterns: identities that do not hold up, timelines that do not fit, and the same names appearing on other claims.

The concern

“A R1.5 million claim just landed on our desk. Is this person even real, and did it happen the way they say?”

What we find out

  • Whether the claimant's identity holds up
  • Whether the incident, dates and places are consistent with what is publicly visible
  • Links to previous or related claims, entities and people
  • Business interests and assets relevant to the claim
  • Inconsistencies between statements, documents and public records

Available by arrangement. Tell us about the claim and we will confirm how and when we can assist.

How we work

Thorough. Documented. Report-based.

Step 1

Consultation

You tell us what is at stake, in confidence. We agree the questions that need answers, the scope and the fee, in writing.

Step 2

Written instruction

Before any work starts, you confirm who you are, your purpose, and that the findings will be used only for that purpose. South African law requires it, and it protects you.

Step 3

In-depth research

Official registers, accredited bureaus and public sources, with every step recorded. We never access accounts, systems or devices, never intercept communications, and never use false pretences.

Step 4

Written report

Findings, sources and our assessment in plain language. Records are destroyed once the purpose is fulfilled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

We act in good faith and hold every engagement to the Protection of Personal Information Act, the Private Security Industry Regulation Act and the PSIRA Code of Conduct, the Cybercrimes Act, RICA, the National Credit Act and the Protection from Harassment Act, so our findings stand up wherever you need to use them. Scope is confirmed in a written service agreement.
