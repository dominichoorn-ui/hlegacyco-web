Suppliers and bidders

Know who you're really doing business with

The lowest price is attractive, until the company behind it turns out to be a front, a renamed version of a business that failed on its last contract, or connected to someone on your own payroll. By then the contract is signed and the money is moving.

Book a consultationAll for businesses services

The concern

“They're the cheapest bid by R2 million. So who's really behind this company, and what's it going to cost us if we're wrong?”

What we find out

  • Who is registered as director and, where it can be established, who owns and controls the company
  • Other companies those people are or were linked to, including deregistered and renamed businesses
  • Company status, age and trading history, checked against the claims in the bid
  • Links between the company's people and your own staff or decision-makers, where you provide staff details
  • Adverse media, litigation, published regulator findings, and what past clients say on reputable social media platforms
  • Sanctions and politically exposed person (PEP) screening

What you receive

A written report

A supplier verification report: findings, sources, a red / amber / green risk rating, and the questions to put to the bidder before you award.

How we work

Thorough. Documented. Report-based.

Step 1

Consultation

You tell us what is at stake, in confidence. We agree the questions that need answers, the scope and the fee, in writing.

Step 2

Written instruction

Before any work starts, you confirm who you are, your purpose, and that the findings will be used only for that purpose. South African law requires it, and it protects you.

Step 3

In-depth research

Official registers, accredited bureaus and public sources, with every step recorded. We never access accounts, systems or devices, never intercept communications, and never use false pretences.

Step 4

Written report

Findings, sources and our assessment in plain language. Records are destroyed once the purpose is fulfilled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

We act in good faith and hold every engagement to the Protection of Personal Information Act, the Private Security Industry Regulation Act and the PSIRA Code of Conduct, the Cybercrimes Act, RICA, the National Credit Act and the Protection from Harassment Act, so our findings stand up wherever you need to use them. Scope is confirmed in a written service agreement.
