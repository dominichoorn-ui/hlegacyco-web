The depth behind every report

State-grade research. Due-diligence depth.

Every report draws on reputable South African government registers, regulators and accredited bureaus: the depth of research a state investigation or a bank’s due diligence relies on, read and interpreted by an accredited forensic investigator and held to the standard of evidence.

Official registers

  • Company and director records
  • Construction and home-builder registers
  • Official government notices
  • Government restricted-supplier lists

People, with consent

  • National identity verification
  • Fingerprint criminal record checks
  • Credit bureau reports
  • Qualification, employment and residence verification

Property, money and assets

  • Property ownership, bonds and transfers
  • Vehicle finance and history
  • Financial-sector and credit-regulator authorisations

Courts, regulators and reputation

  • Court records and judgments
  • Industry-regulator registrations
  • Sanctions lists and regulator findings
  • Reputable social media platforms and public online sources

How we work

Thorough. Documented. Report-based.

Step 1

Consultation

You tell us what is at stake, in confidence. We agree the questions that need answers, the scope and the fee, in writing.

Step 2

Written instruction

Before any work starts, you confirm who you are, your purpose, and that the findings will be used only for that purpose. South African law requires it, and it protects you.

Step 3

In-depth research

Official registers, accredited bureaus and public sources, with every step recorded. We never access accounts, systems or devices, never intercept communications, and never use false pretences.

Step 4

Written report

Findings, sources and our assessment in plain language. Records are destroyed once the purpose is fulfilled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

We act in good faith and hold every engagement to the Protection of Personal Information Act, the Private Security Industry Regulation Act and the PSIRA Code of Conduct, the Cybercrimes Act, RICA, the National Credit Act and the Protection from Harassment Act, so our findings stand up wherever you need to use them. Scope is confirmed in a written service agreement.

Our limits

What we will not do.

Our reports are trusted because of how they are made. These lines are not negotiable, whoever the client and whatever the fee.

  • Access anyone's accounts, devices or systems, or intercept their messages or calls
  • Pose as someone else or use false pretences to obtain information
  • Help anyone confront, expose, harass or retaliate against a person
  • Publish or share findings beyond the purpose you instructed us on
  • Collect debts. Recovery is done by you, your attorney or a registered debt collector
  • Take on, or continue, a matter where we suspect deception or an unlawful purpose
